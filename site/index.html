what you get

dotfilesone command

Status, Tools, Files, Look and Machine in one terminal UI. Every action is a plain dotfiles subcommand too.

pinnedmise

37 tools at exact versions with locked checksums, the same on every machine. Renovate keeps them current.

themedbase16

328 palettes across the terminal, Neovim, bat, lazygit, btop and more. theme, glass and wallpaper switch live.

your pickinstall

The installer asks: everything, core only, or groups and single tools. Change your mind later in the Tools tab.

healthydoctor · update

dotfiles update pulls, provisions and verifies. doctor explains anything off and how to fix it.

yoursno sudo needed

Installs into your home without root. Signed GitHub commits and age-encrypted secrets are built in.
